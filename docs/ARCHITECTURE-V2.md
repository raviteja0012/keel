## 0. The governing sentence, corrected

`ARCHITECTURE.md` §0 says:

> A strategy decides what it wants. The engine decides what actually happens.

That is true about **code topology** and false about **state, identity and authority**.
The corrected version, which the rest of this document implements:

> **A strategy decides what it wants. The engine decides what actually happens —
> the engine only believes what the venue confirms, and the engine is the only thing
> that may write an order.**

Three clauses, three verified breaches:

| Clause | Breach | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| the engine decides | `news_agent.py` is a second OS process with independent authority to close live positions and rewrite live stops | `news_agent.py:378` POSTs to `/api/commands`; `server.py:144-217` enqueues `close_trade` and `trail_sl` for any ticket, **unauthenticated** |
| only believes what the venue confirms | the engine writes `tp1_done`, `sl` and `status='closed'` optimistically, before any confirmation | `engine.py:770-793` (stop written at enqueue), `engine.py:794-806` (`pnl=0.0, exit_reason='closed at broker'` on mere absence) |
| the engine decides | the rails read their thresholds out of the same dict object a strategy already had | `engine.py:865` `p = params()` → passed to `strategies.generate_all(...)` at `:926` **and** to `try_execute(sig, p)` at `:948`; `try_execute` reads `p["risk_pct"]` at `:436` |

And one clause the document never had, which turns out to be load-bearing:

> **Identity is the venue's ticket, not a text field the counterparty may rewrite.**

### 2.2 Adopted — genuinely new findings the synthesis missed

These change the recommendation because they are defects nobody had named.

1. **Ticket truncation (W1).** `SLCDataBridge.mq5:989/1031/1032` cast MT5 `ulong` tickets
   through MQL5 32-bit `int`. Live tickets exceed 2³¹ and wrap negative.
   `manage_open_trades`'s `x.get("ticket") == tr["ticket"]` never matches;
   `close_trade{ticket:-1234}` fails `PositionSelectByTicket` and the EA acks it as gone;
   `position_id` sums match nothing. **Demo certifies green, live fails.** This single
   defect invalidates most of the live-path work if it is not fixed first.

2. **The gate is per-cell, the switch it guards is global.** `live_switch._open_cells()`
   (`:56-58`) returns *any* open cell; `request_live` refuses only if the set is **empty**
   (`:118-125`); `confirm_live` sets one global `trading_mode='live'` (`:159`). One open
   cell authorises live trading for every strategy, every class, every symbol. Extending
   the cell key (BL-8) makes this *worse* — more independent chances to open the global
   switch. **Fix: promotion is an allow-list, not a mode** (§4.4).

3. **Stage and execution mode are different axes and the design conflates them.**
   `try_execute` reads `mode = p["trading_mode"]` at `:321` — a single global setting — and
   writes it into the trade row at `:487`. A strategy at stage PAPER routes to
   `try_execute`, passes SF-4, and places a **live** order because the global switch is on.
   Its trades are written `mode='live'` and never accumulate in `analysis._closed("paper")`,
   so its own gate cell stays empty forever while it spends real money. **Fix: lattice
   minimum** (§4.3).

4. **`/api/commands` is a live stop-widening path outside `engine.py`.**
   `server.py:144-217` is unauthenticated. Its docstring says "Only stop-tightening
   commands are allowed"; the next line accepts `close_trade`. For a live ticket the
   command is `enqueue_command`ed **before** the tighten-only comparison, and that
   comparison only mutates the local mirror. The sole live tighten-only enforcement is
   `ExecuteSLModify`, guarded by `&& current_sl > 0` (`SLCDataBridge.mq5:833-842`) — so any
   position with **no** stop attached accepts an arbitrary `new_sl`, including one on the
   far side of price. Direct violation of invariant 3, reachable from the LAN, today.

5. **`news_agent` is a second execution authority.** `news_evaluator.py:544` emits
   `close_trade` from RSS sentiment scoring; `news_agent.py:636` POSTs it. Compounding:
   a news cut makes the position disappear, `manage_open_trades`'s else-branch writes
   `pnl=0.0, exit_reason='closed at broker'` (`:794-806`), and `loss_governor`'s
   `(r["pnl"] or 0) < 0` test (`:275`) counts `0.0` as a **win** — resetting the
   consecutive-loss streak and un-halving risk. **Invariant 10 defeated by `0.0` instead
   of `NULL`**, by the exact mechanism the comment at `:267-269` warns about.
   Also: `server.py:171` calls `engine._close_paper` from the Flask thread, racing
   `manage_open_trades` on the same row — a real concurrency bug in **paper mode today**.

6. **`origin='system'` does not exist.** `params_store.WHITELISTS` has keys
   `agent/sanity/news/human` only (`:53-67`), while `_AUTOMATED` already lists `"system"`
   (`:69`) — which makes the omission look intentional. Any automated de-risking write
   raises `ParamRejected("unknown origin")`. And because `"system"` is in `_AUTOMATED`, the
   human pin (`:121-125`) would block it for 7 days after any manual halt/resume anyway.
   **Both automatic safety mechanisms are inert as designed.**

7. **Feed staleness is not a rail.** The `feed_age > 60` stand-aside lives in `engine_loop`
   (`:871-878`), not in `try_execute`. `POST /api/tv_webhook` → `ingest_external_signal` →
   `try_execute` (`:604`) runs on a Flask worker with no age check, sizing off
   `feed_state["prices"]` which can be arbitrarily stale. The spread and drift rails both
   compute against the same stale quote, so they agree with themselves.

8. **External signals have no stage.** `ingest_external_signal` mints
   `strategy = "ext:%s" % src` from a provider-supplied field (`:590, 602`). Those cells
   never accumulate 50 paper trades and never get a sign-off, yet once the global switch is
   live they place live orders. Under any registry, `effective_stage("ext:tradingview")` is
   undefined, and this repo's established default is default-ON
   (`strategies/__init__.py:23`) — which is G1, the gap the whole architecture exists to
   close.

9. **`enabled_pairs` / `watch_pairs` / `modes` bypass bounds and audit.** They are in
   `server.py`'s `allowed_direct` (`:329`) and **not** in `params_store.BOUNDS`, so
   `guarded = set(BOUNDS) | {"min_grade"}` (`:338`) misses them and they take the direct
   `storage.set_setting` branch: no bounds, no `param_changes` row, no sign-off
   invalidation, no auth. Moving a symbol from `watch_pairs` to `enabled_pairs` promotes it
   from SHADOW to real money in one unaudited POST, inheriting a sign-off earned by
   different instruments. Adding `swing` to `modes` doubles effective concurrency.

10. **Late acks are rejected and the trade is orphaned.** `next_command` expires rows in
    `sent` state (`storage.py:367-369`) — i.e. a command the EA is executing right now —
    and `ack_command` only updates `status IN ('pending','sent')` (`:386-388`). With
    `PollCommandsSec=10`, a 5s WebRequest timeout, and the EA rebuilding **30 days** of deal
    history on every 5s push (`ClosedTradesDays = 30`, `mq5:37`), late acks are ordinary.

11. **`state/` is cwd-relative while the DB is not.** `_DB_PATH` is `__file__`-derived
    (`storage.py:13`); `os.makedirs("state")` at `engine.py:98` and `:821` is not. A
    launchd job's cwd is not guaranteed, so any filesystem sentinel placed in the repo
    directory may be invisible to the engine.

12. **`venues.py` does not exist.** `dashboard_api.py:187-222` lazily imports it in five
    handlers. `/api/venues` currently 500s. The `brokers/` package was added without its
    credential store.

## 4. The definitive strategy-plugin interface

Design rules, each earned by a defect above:

- **No auto-discovery.** SF-7 is killed (§10). One author, one line in `REGISTRY`.
  Import-time discovery buys nothing here and makes "conformance gates registration" false.
- **No purity clause.** Python cannot check it, and Nautilus, Freqtrade and Hummingbot all
  get the guarantee by *never handing the strategy the capability*. Same here.
- **Absolute risk caps, not weights.** Renormalising by active weights raises survivors'
  risk when one strategy is disabled — a factor that increases risk.
- **Stage is not execution mode.** The effective mode is the lattice minimum.
- **An unknown strategy id resolves to DISABLED**, asserted in the conformance suite,
  because the failure is silent.

```python
# trading-bot/strategies/spec.py
"""What a strategy declares. It declares; it never decides.

Nothing here can widen a stop, raise risk, choose an execution mode, or reach a
venue. A plugin holds no reference to `storage`, to `engine`, or to the mutable
global params dict — not by convention, but because StrategyContext is the only
thing it is handed.
"""
from __future__ import annotations

import enum
from dataclasses import dataclass, field
from types import MappingProxyType
from typing import Any, Iterable, Mapping, Optional, Protocol, Sequence

import params_store


class Stage(enum.IntEnum):
    """Ordered so `min()` is the lattice meet. Never reorder; never insert."""
    DISABLED = 0    # not invoked at all
    SHADOW   = 1    # signals recorded, never executed, never notified
    PAPER    = 2    # executes against the paper broker, all rails
    LIVE     = 3    # real money — reachable only via live_switch, per cell

    @classmethod
    def parse(cls, s: Optional[str]) -> "Stage":
        """Unknown, missing or malformed -> DISABLED. Fails closed by design:
        `ext:tradingview` and any typo land here."""
        try:
            return cls[str(s).strip().upper()]
        except (KeyError, AttributeError):
            return cls.DISABLED


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class ParamDecl:
    """One namespaced parameter. Type, bounds and default are written ONCE and
    drive params_store.BOUNDS, the conformance suite and the 8767 form."""
    default: float
    lo: float
    hi: float
    doc: str = ""

    def validate(self) -> None:
        if not (self.lo <= self.default <= self.hi):
            raise ValueError("default %s outside [%s, %s]" % (self.default, self.lo, self.hi))


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class StrategySpec:
    name: str                                  # stable id; appears in every DB row
    version: str                               # bump on behaviour change -> voids sign-off
    description: str
    asset_classes: frozenset                   # engine will not invoke outside these
    trade_modes: frozenset                     # {"intraday", "swing"}
    timeframes: tuple                          # bars required; drives the fetch, not a label
    params: Mapping[str, ParamDecl] = field(default_factory=dict)

    # ---- risk: ABSOLUTE, never a share to be renormalised -------------------
    max_risk_pct: float = 1.0                  # hard per-trade cap for THIS strategy
    max_open_positions: int = 2                # this strategy's own concurrency cap
    max_heat_pct: float = 1.0                  # this strategy's own summed-open-risk cap

    # ---- lifecycle ----------------------------------------------------------
    max_stage: Stage = Stage.PAPER             # CEILING the author may not exceed

    # ---- observability contract (consumed by the liveness monitor) ----------
    expected_signals_per_day: tuple = (0.0, 50.0)
    max_silence_s: int = 6 * 3600
    warmup_bars: int = 200

    def validate(self) -> None:
        """Raised at import. A spec that does not validate is not registered."""
        if not self.name.isidentifier():
            raise ValueError("strategy name must be a bare identifier: %r" % self.name)
        if self.name.startswith("ext"):
            raise ValueError("'ext' prefix is reserved for external intake")
        if not (0 < self.max_risk_pct <= params_store.RISK_PCT_CEILING):
            raise ValueError("max_risk_pct must be in (0, %s]" % params_store.RISK_PCT_CEILING)
        if self.max_heat_pct > params_store.PORTFOLIO_HEAT_CEILING:
            raise ValueError("max_heat_pct above the portfolio ceiling")
        if self.max_stage is Stage.LIVE:
            raise ValueError("a spec may not declare max_stage=LIVE; live is per-cell "
                             "and granted only by live_switch")
        for k, d in self.params.items():
            if not k.islower() or "." in k:
                raise ValueError("param key %r must be a bare lowercase key" % k)
            d.validate()

    def qualified(self, key: str) -> str:
        return "strategy.%s.%s" % (self.name, key)


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class StrategyContext:
    """Everything a strategy may see. Deliberately does NOT carry `storage`,
    `engine`, the global params dict, the account balance, open positions, or
    the trading mode. A strategy that cannot see risk state cannot reason about
    it, which is the point."""
    symbol: str
    asset_class: str
    trade_mode: str                            # "intraday" | "swing"
    bars: Mapping[str, Sequence[Mapping[str, Any]]]   # tf -> bars, oldest first
    spread: float
    live_price: Optional[float]
    now: float                                 # engine clock, seconds
    broker_now: float                          # broker clock (bars are broker time)
    config: Mapping[str, float]                # THIS strategy's namespaced params, frozen

    @staticmethod
    def freeze(d: Mapping[str, Any]) -> Mapping[str, Any]:
        return MappingProxyType(dict(d))


class Strategy(Protocol):
    """Implement two things. Everything else is the engine's job."""
    SPEC: StrategySpec

    def generate(self, ctx: StrategyContext) -> Mapping[str, Any]:
        """Return {"signal": <sig or None>, "info": {...}}.

        `sig` must carry: side, entry, sl, tp1, tp, rr, grade, key, setup.
        The engine overwrites `strategy`, re-prices `entry` at the live book,
        re-checks RR at the actual fill, and sizes the position. A stop is
        mandatory; a signal without one is refused at the choke point.
        """
```

```python
# trading-bot/strategies/registry.py
"""Explicit registry. One line per strategy. No import-time discovery."""
from typing import Dict, List, Optional

import storage
from .spec import Stage, StrategySpec
from .slc import SLC_SPEC, SLCStrategy

REGISTRY: Dict[str, object] = {
    SLC_SPEC.name: SLCStrategy(),
}

# External intake is a first-class registered pseudo-strategy with its own
# spec, stage, gate cell and risk cap — not a string minted from a webhook body.
EXTERNAL_NAME = "external_intake"


def spec(name: str) -> Optional[StrategySpec]:
    s = REGISTRY.get(name)
    return getattr(s, "SPEC", None) if s else None


def effective_stage(name: str) -> Stage:
    """DB stage, clamped by the spec ceiling. Unknown name -> DISABLED.

    LIVE is NOT reachable here. `live_switch` grants live per cell in the
    `live_cells` table; this function's ceiling is PAPER for everything.
    """
    sp = spec(name)
    if sp is None:
        return Stage.DISABLED
    declared = Stage.parse(storage.get_setting("strategy_%s_stage" % name, "shadow"))
    return Stage(min(int(declared), int(sp.max_stage)))


def active(trade_mode: str, asset_class: str) -> List[str]:
    out = []
    for name, impl in REGISTRY.items():
        sp = impl.SPEC
        if effective_stage(name) is Stage.DISABLED:
            continue
        if trade_mode not in sp.trade_modes or asset_class not in sp.asset_classes:
            continue
        out.append(name)
    return out


def required_timeframes() -> frozenset:
    """Drives the engine's bar fetch. Replaces the hardcoded tuple at
    engine.py:904-905, which is why SF-6's `timeframes` field is currently
    a passive label."""
    tfs: set = set()
    for impl in REGISTRY.values():
        tfs.update(impl.SPEC.timeframes)
    return frozenset(tfs)
```

### 4.3 Execution mode is the lattice minimum — the fix for D3

```python
# engine.py, top of try_execute — replaces `mode = p["trading_mode"]` at :321
def resolve_exec_mode(strategy_name: str, symbol: str, p: Dict) -> str:
    """LIVE requires THREE independent yeses:
         1. the strategy's effective stage is LIVE-eligible (registry ceiling),
         2. this exact (strategy, asset_class, venue) cell is in `live_cells`,
         3. the global master switch is 'live'.
       Anything short of all three degrades to paper. The global switch may
       VETO live; it may never GRANT it."""
    global_mode = p["trading_mode"]                    # live | paper | off
    if global_mode == "off":
        return "off"
    stage = registry.effective_stage(strategy_name)
    if stage is Stage.DISABLED:
        return "off"
    if stage is Stage.SHADOW:
        return "shadow"
    cell_live = live_switch.cell_is_live(
        strategy_name, instruments.asset_class(symbol), venue="mt5")
    return "live" if (global_mode == "live" and cell_live) else "paper"
```

Everywhere `mode` is used today — the rails, `_balance`, `loss_limits_hit`, the trade row

### 4.4 Promotion is an allow-list — the fix for D2

```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS live_cells (
    strategy TEXT NOT NULL, asset_class TEXT NOT NULL, venue TEXT NOT NULL,
    granted_t INTEGER NOT NULL, granted_by TEXT NOT NULL,
    signoff_id INTEGER, sample_epoch INTEGER NOT NULL,
    revoked_t INTEGER,
    PRIMARY KEY (strategy, asset_class, venue)
);
```

`POST /api/live/confirm` must **name** the cell it is promoting, re-validate that specific
cell at confirm time (not "any cell"), and write one row. `trading_mode` remains a global
master OFF switch: it can veto, never grant. De-escalation revokes rows and is never gated.

`sample_epoch` is a monotonic integer, incremented by `reset_ledger.py` and by any bulk
delete of `trades`, stamped on every trade row and every sign-off. A sign-off from a stale
epoch does not count, and the gate screen shows the epoch — a cell on epoch 4 is a
different claim from a cell on epoch 1. Without it, "run 50, adjust, reset, run 50" is a
resample-until-significant machine and nothing records how many samples were drawn.

### 4.5 Conformance suite — only what is checkable

Replaces SF-9. Runs in CI against every entry in `REGISTRY`:

1. `SPEC.validate()` passes.
2. `generate()` returns the `{signal, info}` shape; a returned signal carries a stop on the
   correct side of entry, `rr > 0`, and a non-empty `key`.
3. **Non-mutation:** `ctx.config` is a `MappingProxyType`; a write raises `TypeError`.
   Asserted by a hostile plugin fixture that tries `ctx.config["risk_pct"] = 5.0`.
4. **Determinism:** identical `ctx` → byte-identical signal, twice, in a fresh interpreter.
5. **Scope:** never emits for an asset class or trade mode outside its declared sets
   (property test over the instrument registry).
6. `SPEC.max_stage is not Stage.LIVE`.
7. `effective_stage("no_such_strategy") is Stage.DISABLED` and `try_execute` refuses it.
   Its own test, because the failure is silent.
8. Every declared param key resolves to a `params_store.BOUNDS` entry generated from the
   declaration.

## 5. The definitive BrokerAdapter interface

`trading-bot/brokers/__init__.py` already exists and is 80% right. Three things in it are
wrong and must change before any adapter is trusted with money.

### 5.1 What is wrong with the current file

**`place_order` "MUST be idempotent on order.client_order_id. Resubmitting the same id must
return the existing order, never create a second one."** (`brokers/__init__.py:140-142`)

No adapter can honour this, because the same field means four different things:

| Venue | Behaviour of a reused client id |
|---|---|
| Alpaca | rejected while the order is active; **reusable once terminal** |
| Binance (`newClientOrderId`) | rejected until filled/expired, then **lapses at exactly the moment you need it** |
| IBKR (`orderId`) | reusing it **modifies the live order** |
| FIX (`ClOrdID`) | a fresh id is required per attempt, with `PossDupFlag(43)` set explicitly |
| MT5 EA bridge | no field at all — the order comment is the only carrier, and brokers rewrite it |

Stated as an adapter guarantee, it invites a generic retry wrapper. **The generic retry
wrapper is the mechanism that manufactures double fills.** `ccxt_venue.place_order` already
half-knows this — it looks before retrying on untrusted venues (`:199-205`) and raises
`VenueError(retryable=True)` when a network error leaves the outcome unknown (`:224`). But
`retryable=True` on a *submit* is exactly the wrong signal to hand a caller.

**`OrderResult.status` has an `unknown` string but nothing forces the caller to handle it.**

**`VenueHealth` carries `clock_skew_s` and nothing consumes it.**

### 5.2 The corrected contract

```python
# trading-bot/brokers/__init__.py  — replacing the Protocol section
import enum
from dataclasses import dataclass, field
from typing import Any, Dict, Iterator, List, Optional, Protocol, runtime_checkable


class IdempotencyModel(enum.Enum):
    """How the venue treats a repeated client_order_id. The engine reads this
    and CHANGES ITS BEHAVIOUR; it is not documentation."""
    NONE                 = "none"                  # ids ignored entirely
    DEDUP_WHILE_ACTIVE   = "dedup_while_active"    # Alpaca: reusable once terminal
    DEDUP_UNTIL_TERMINAL = "dedup_until_terminal"  # Binance: lapses when it matters
    REUSE_IS_MODIFY      = "reuse_is_modify"       # IBKR: reuse AMENDS the live order
    FRESH_ID_PER_ATTEMPT = "fresh_id_per_attempt"  # FIX: new ClOrdID + PossDupFlag


class Outcome(enum.Enum):
    ACCEPTED = "accepted"     # the venue has it
    REJECTED = "rejected"     # the venue refused it, permanently
    UNKNOWN  = "unknown"      # WE DO NOT KNOW. Resolve by READING, never by writing.


class PositionModel(enum.Enum):
    NETTING = "netting"       # MT5 default, spot crypto
    HEDGING = "hedging"       # MT5 hedging accounts, most futures


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class AdapterCapabilities:
    idempotency: IdempotencyModel
    can_lookup_by_client_id: bool
    supports_attached_stop: bool      # stop rests AT THE VENUE, survives our death
    atomic_stop_amend: bool
    position_model: PositionModel
    environment: str                  # "demo" | "testnet" | "live" — part of the gate cell
    max_clock_skew_ms: int = 5_000
    min_stop_distance_supported: bool = False
    supports_fill_lookup: bool = True
    rate_budget_per_min: int = 60


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class SubmitResult:
    """Deliberately not called OrderResult. A submit produces an OUTCOME, and
    one of the three outcomes is 'I do not know'."""
    client_order_id: str
    outcome: Outcome
    venue_order_id: str = ""
    filled_qty: float = 0.0
    avg_price: Optional[float] = None
    venue_code: str = ""              # retcode / error code, verbatim
    message: str = ""
    raw: Dict[str, Any] = field(default_factory=dict)


@runtime_checkable
class BrokerAdapter(Protocol):
    name: str
    read_only: bool

    def capabilities(self) -> AdapterCapabilities:
        """Static. The engine REFUSES to route LIVE to an adapter where
        `idempotency is NONE and not can_lookup_by_client_id` — there is no
        way to make submission safe on such a venue."""

    def health(self) -> "VenueHealth":
        """Never raises. A dead venue is a health result."""

    def clock(self) -> float:
        """Venue's own clock, epoch seconds. Skew above
        capabilities.max_clock_skew_ms is a pre-trade stand-aside."""

    def symbol_meta(self, symbol: str) -> "SymbolMeta": ...
    def balances(self) -> List["Balance"]: ...
    def positions(self) -> List["Position"]: ...

    # ---------------------------------------------------------------- writes
    def submit(self, order: "Order") -> SubmitResult:
        """Attempted EXACTLY ONCE. Never retried by the adapter, never retried
        by the engine. Any transport failure, timeout or ambiguous venue code
        returns Outcome.UNKNOWN and the reconciler resolves it by reading.

        This method is the only one in the interface that may create risk."""

    def amend_stop(self, venue_position_id: str, new_stop: float) -> SubmitResult:
        """Tighten-only is enforced by the ENGINE against the confirmed stop.
        An adapter that cannot express 'tighten only' must still refuse a
        widening amend, but the engine never relies on that."""

    def cancel(self, venue_order_id: str, symbol: str = "") -> bool: ...

    # ------------------------------------------------- ambiguity resolution
    def order_by_client_id(self, client_order_id: str) -> Optional[SubmitResult]:
        """The read that resolves UNKNOWN. Required whenever
        capabilities.idempotency is NONE."""

    def recent_fills(self, since_ts: float) -> List[Dict[str, Any]]:
        """Every fill, not just ours. The drop-copy side of reconciliation."""

    def stream_prices(self, symbols: List[str]) -> Iterator["Tick"]: ...
```

### 5.3 The submission protocol — buildable today, no adapters required

The protocol lives in the **engine**, not in adapters. That is the whole point: the engine
gets one safety story regardless of what any venue does.

```
client_order_id = f"{strategy}-{trade_id}-{attempt}"

1. INSERT into `orders` (state='intended', client_order_id, payload)
   and COMMIT — before any network call, before any queue row.
2. Submit exactly once.
3. ACCEPTED -> state='submitted'.  REJECTED -> state='rejected', decisions row.
   UNKNOWN  -> state='unknown', halt_new_entries, page. Do not write again.
4. The reconciler resolves 'unknown' by READING: order_by_client_id,
   recent_fills, the position feed. Only reads may be retried, with backoff.
5. On startup, trading is BLOCKED until every 'intended' and 'unknown' row is
   resolved. This closes restart amnesia.
```

**Reads may retry with jittered backoff. Submits get exactly one attempt, ever.** That
sentence is the adapter conformance suite's central assertion.

### 5.4 What MT5 needs to satisfy this

The EA bridge is already a drop copy — it pushes `open_positions[]` with ticket, lots,
**sl** and comment every 5s (`mq5:977-1005`), read from the terminal's own tables
independently of the command/ack path. It needs four changes:

1. **`(long)` casts** (W1) — otherwise every identity below is wrong on live.
2. **The ack carries an outcome.** Today `AcknowledgeCommand` posts an empty body and the
   EA acks only `if(ok)`. Instead: ack **unconditionally**, with
   `{retcode, ticket: g_trade.ResultOrder(), filled_lots, position_id}`. Three buckets on
   the server: `10008/10009` → `done`; `10014/10015/10016/10019` → `rejected` (permanent,
   with the code recorded); `10010/10012/10013` or WebRequest failure → `unknown`. This
   single change makes D6, D7, D8, D9 and D14 diagnosable.
3. **Identity is the ticket.** Store the acked ticket on the trade row before any further
   command. Comments are 31 characters and brokers rewrite, truncate or strip them —
   anchoring on `"SLC#%d" in comment` (`engine.py:204`, `:765`) means a bridge that appends
   `[sl 1.0850]` on a stop-out turns your own position into `VENUE_ONLY_FOREIGN`. Keep the
   comment as a fallback hint only; a NULL ticket after ack is a paged condition.
4. **Refuse, don't clamp.** `mq5:736-738` runs `MathMin(lots, MaxLotsPerTrade)` then
   `MathMin(lots, maxLot)` then `MathMax(lots, minLot)` — the floor runs **last**, so on any
   symbol where `minLot > MaxLotsPerTrade` the EA's own hard cap is silently overridden by
   the minimum-lot floor. **Floors first, caps last, and a floor that would violate a cap is
   a refusal.** Report the lots actually used in the ack, so `trades.lots` records reality
   rather than the request.

### 5.5 Auth and reconciliation

**Authenticate the whole plane, not half of it.** One `@app.before_request` shared-secret
check (secret in the runtime DB per PL-5, EA input) covering `/api/mt5_feed`,
`/api/mt5_bars`, `/api/commands/next`, `/api/commands/ack/*` **and `/api/commands`,
`/api/trade/close/*`, `/api/halt`, `/api/resume`, `/api/settings`**. Bind to the tailnet
address rather than `0.0.0.0` (`config.yaml:9`), with a bind retry — if `tailscaled` is not
up at boot, Flask fails to bind and the server does not start.

**Reconciliation is 40 lines against data already in memory.** Each cycle, from
`feed_state["open_positions"]`, **filtered to magic 770001**:

| Class | Action |
|---|---|
| `LOCAL_ONLY` (we think it is open, venue does not report it) | `recon_state='unconfirmed'`, keep `status='open'`, keep managing, keep counting risk, page |
| `VENUE_ONLY_TAGGED` (our magic, no local row) | `halt_new_entries`, page — this is the double fill |
| `VENUE_ONLY_FOREIGN` (other magic) | **alert only, never halt** — the owner will hold manual positions |
| `QTY_MISMATCH` | tolerate for one cycle (partial-close race), then page |
| `STOP_MISMATCH` (`abs(pos["sl"] - tr["sl"]) > tick`) | page; this replaces the `sl_confirmed` column |

**Never auto-heal by placing orders.** Halting rather than healing keeps reconciliation
inside "factors may only reduce risk". And validate the feed body first — distinguish an
absent `open_positions` key from an empty list, reject bodies lacking `account`/`terminal`
— or one malformed push (D31) classifies every live position as LOCAL_ONLY.

## 6. Risk-budget sizing for N concurrent strategies

Replaces `ARCHITECTURE.md` §2.4, whose formula
`risk_pct * (weight / sum(active weights))` **raises risk** when a strategy is disabled:
three equal-weight strategies at 33% become two at 50%.

### 6.1 The formula

```python
# trading-bot/risk.py  — pure functions. The engine is the only thing that ACTS
# on them; the dashboard imports the same code so the displayed number and the
# enforced number cannot diverge.

MIN_VIABLE_RISK_PCT = 0.10          # below this, stand aside — never micro-size

def intended_risk_pct(spec, p, grade, gov, sess_factor, m_vol, m_regime) -> float:
    """Step 1 — the strategy's own intended size, monotonically de-risked.

    ABSOLUTE cap, never renormalised: disabling one strategy changes nothing
    about any other strategy's size.
    """
    base = min(spec.max_risk_pct, p["risk_pct"], params_store.RISK_PCT_CEILING)
    factors = (
        p["b_setup_risk_factor"] if grade == "B" else 1.0,
        gov,             # loss governor, per strategy      -> {0.5, 1.0}
        sess_factor,     # session (e.g. crypto weekend)    -> (0, 1]
        m_regime,        # clip(regime_b_ban / atr_ratio, floor, 1.0)
        m_vol,           # clip(1.0% / sigma_20d, floor, 1.0), 1.0 below 20 days
    )
    for f in factors:
        assert 0.0 < f <= 1.0, "risk factors may only reduce risk"
        base *= f
    return base


def headroom_pct(candidate, open_trades, spec, p) -> Dict[str, float]:
    """Step 2 — three independent ceilings, all in absolute account-percent.

    Heat is computed from the LIVE stop distance, not the recorded entry
    risk_pct: after TP1 the stop is at breakeven, so real open risk is <= 0
    while `trades.risk_pct` still reports full entry risk. Without this the
    ceiling binds hardest exactly when the book is winning.
    """
    def live_heat(t) -> float:
        stop = t["sl"]                     # confirmed stop
        if t["side"] == "buy":
            adverse = max(0.0, t["entry"] - stop)
        else:
            adverse = max(0.0, stop - t["entry"])
        return adverse / abs(t["entry"] - t["initial_sl"]) * t["risk_pct"] \
            if t["initial_sl"] != t["entry"] else 0.0

    portfolio = sum(live_heat(t) for t in open_trades)
    per_strat = sum(live_heat(t) for t in open_trades if t["strategy"] == spec.name)
    bucket    = exposure.worst_bucket_risk_pct(open_trades, candidate)  # ABSOLUTE %

    return {
        "portfolio": p["max_portfolio_heat_pct"] - portfolio,   # default 1.0%
        "strategy":  spec.max_heat_pct          - per_strat,
        "bucket":    p["max_bucket_risk_pct"]   - bucket,       # default 1.0%
    }


def final_risk_pct(intended, headroom) -> Tuple[Optional[float], str]:
    """Step 3 — REFUSE, do not shrink.

    Clipping to remaining headroom turns a ceiling into a target: the book runs
    permanently at 100% heat and the marginal trade is sized below the level at
    which its expectancy was measured — the same sample poisoning that the TP1
    fill-model divergence causes. Shrink is permitted only down to 0.75x, and
    those trades are tagged so they can be excluded from the gate sample.
    """
    cap = min(headroom.values())
    if cap <= 0:
        return None, "portfolio_heat"
    if intended <= cap:
        return intended, ""
    if cap >= 0.75 * intended:
        return cap, "shrunk"                    # tagged: excluded from the gate
    return None, "portfolio_heat"               # skip(track=True) -> shadow sample


def viable(risk_pct, balance, symbol, entry, sl) -> Tuple[bool, str]:
    """Step 4 — the size floor is a POLICY decision, not a sizing failure.

    No 0.25x floor on the de-risking stack: a floor on a product of
    strictly-reducing factors is a factor that RAISES risk, and it binds exactly
    when the governor, the regime scalar and the vol scalar all fire together.
    """
    if risk_pct < MIN_VIABLE_RISK_PCT:
        return False, "below size floor (%.3f%% < %.2f%%)" % (risk_pct, MIN_VIABLE_RISK_PCT)
    lots, actual = calc_lots(symbol, entry, sl, balance * risk_pct / 100.0)
    if lots <= 0:
        return False, "no tick metadata"
    if actual > balance * risk_pct / 100.0 * 1.5:
        return False, "min lot would risk %.1fx intended" % (actual / (balance * risk_pct / 100.0))
    return True, ""
```

### 6.2 Constants

| Constant | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `RISK_PCT_CEILING` | 1.0 | unchanged, code constant |
| `PORTFOLIO_HEAT_CEILING` | 2.0 | new code ceiling |
| `max_portfolio_heat_pct` | **1.0** = 0.5 × `daily_stop_pct` | a full-heat, fully-correlated stop-out must leave the operator **inside** the daily limit with room to act. Setting it equal to the stop calibrates the ceiling to be hit — and since heat is denominated in stop distance while realised loss is a gap *through* the stop, the limit is overshot, not approached, with positions still open. |
| `max_bucket_risk_pct` | **1.0**, absolute | replaces `max_bucket_exposure` in normalised weight units. Absolute percent is commensurable with the heat ceiling and the daily stop, and removes the `base_risk_pct` denominator question entirely. |
| `MIN_VIABLE_RISK_PCT` | 0.10 | below this, stand aside with a named state |
| per-strategy `max_risk_pct` | declared, sum-checked | the registry **refuses** a roster whose caps oversubscribe the portfolio ceiling rather than silently rescaling |

### 6.3 The cost you are accepting, written down before it surprises you

Static absolute budgets summing to the portfolio ceiling forgo the diversification
multiplier (Carver's IDM ≈ 2.5 for a broad book). This is **deliberate at zero closed
trades**, because an IDM derived from an unmeasured correlation matrix is leverage built on
a guess, and it is forbidden by "factors may only reduce risk".

The first observation after moving from one strategy at 1% to five summing to 1% will be
that returns fell roughly 1/√N. The tempting fixes — raise `max_concurrent`, raise
`risk_pct` — are exactly the path to 12% heat and 2× Kelly.

**Revisit condition, and only this one:** a measured inter-strategy daily-P&L correlation
matrix over ≥200 closed trades per cell. N strategies at risk r with pairwise ρ give
r·√(N + N(N−1)ρ) — 2.24× at ρ=0, **3.61× at ρ=0.4**. Strategies written in the same repo,
on the same 8 symbols, at the same timeframes, sharing the same ATR machinery and the same
`min_rr` will not be near ρ=0. Report the correlation on the promotion screen from
strategy #2 onward. Surface it; do not block on it.

### 6.4 Two-level loss governor

`loss_governor(mode)` (`engine.py:263-285`) has no strategy filter — a losing streak in B
halves risk for A. Add `AND strategy = ?`. Add a separate **portfolio-level** scalar driven
by rolling account drawdown from the `equity` table (1.0 above −1%, 0.5 down to the daily
stop, 0 at it): a count rule alone misses steady multi-strategy bleed where nobody hits
three in a row, and with five strategies interleaved a count rule fires ~5× as often and
pins the system at half risk permanently.

And: exclude `pnl == 0.0` on **live** rows from the streak (treat as UNKNOWN, streak
preserved) until D6 is fixed. Today a phantom close scores as a win and un-halves risk.

### 6.5 Typed factors

Today the "only reduce" invariant is two hand-written `if gov < 1.0:` guards at
`engine.py:441-444`. That does not survive five more factors written under time pressure.
Define a `RiskFactor` protocol returning a float in (0, 1], a module-level
`ALL_RISK_FACTORS`, fold over the list, and test `0 < f(...) <= 1.0` across a grid plus
`final risk_pct <= RISK_PCT_CEILING` for every combination.

## 7. Alert taxonomy

Today `engine.py:360-362` pushes **every rail rejection** to Telegram *and* Discord at the
same visual weight as TRADE OPENED, gated on `notify_signals` which seeds to `True`. Eight
pairs × two speeds with a 30-minute dedup is roughly 32 messages an hour, bursting exactly
when something is wrong. There is **no action a skip permits** — that is the rail working.

Four tiers. Freqtrade's per-event three-level model (`on` / `silent` / `off`) replaces the
two booleans, with a live preview: *"these settings would have sent N messages in the last
7 days."*

### P1 — PAGE (Telegram + push, bypasses quiet hours)

Something is wrong with **money or truth**, and a human must act now.

| Event | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Reconciliation drift | `VENUE_ONLY_TAGGED`, `QTY_MISMATCH` past one cycle, or `LOCAL_ONLY` on a live row |
| Unconfirmed submit | any `orders` row in `state='unknown'` |
| Unconfirmed stop | requested ≠ confirmed for 2+ cycles on a live position |
| Fill-size mismatch | acked lots ≠ requested lots (the EA clamped) |
| Live heartbeat / feed loss **with open live positions** | `engine_last_cycle_ok_t` stale AND `heartbeat` fresh, or `ea_feed_age_s > 120` |
| DB integrity suspect while live | `storage.integrity_suspect()` non-empty |
| Clock skew | `abs(local_now − terminal.time_utc) > 30s` |
| Negative or wrapped ticket | any ticket ≤ 0 in the feed (the D1 canary — keep it forever) |

### P2 — ALERT (Telegram, one message, no repeat within 30 min)

State changed in a way that changes what the system will do next.

Kill switch armed or fired · loss governor engaged or released · halt set or cleared ·
`trading_mode` change · `live_cells` grant or revoke · strategy stage change ·
`data_trust` verdict flipped or gone stale (>24h) · news-calendar stale · frozen quote ·
`engine_consecutive_errors >= 3` · corruption recovery attempted · a command expired
without an ack · queue depth > 10.

### P3 — DIGEST (one message at 07:00 local)

Trades opened and closed with R · realised and open P&L · gate progress per cell with the
delta since yesterday · top three skip reasons by count with the R those shadow samples
earned or saved · anything P4 that crossed a threshold.

### P4 — LOG ONLY (`decisions` table; visible in the UI, never pushed)

Every rail rejection. Every `no_setup`. Every `stand_aside`. Every dedup hit — which today
is not even logged (D20).

### Delivery

- **One `alerts` table, one relay.** Persist first (transactional outbox), relay drains,
  redelivers on reconnect. Exactly **one** process owns the relay — `server.py`. Every
  other process (`dashboard_api`, `news_agent`, `live_switch`) **inserts only**. Do not call
  `notifier.start()` in a second process: `notifier._q` and `_started` are per-process
  module state (`notifier.py:13-14`), and three relays means three Telegram messages per
  event — the alert-fatigue failure arriving through its own fix.
- Replace `except queue.Full: pass` (`notifier.py:85-86`) with a counter; expose
  `undelivered_alerts` in `health()`.
- Fix `live_switch._notify`'s bare `except: pass` (`:48-53`) — insert an `alerts` row.

### Things that are technically sound and still wrong for this system

Filter: *does the practice exist because of (a) many venues per instrument, (b) many humans
under one book, (c) a regulator, or (d) an unavoidable physical property of networks, clocks
and money?* **Only (d) transfers.** None of (a)(b)(c) hold: one book, one venue per symbol,
no FIX venue anywhere in the roadmap (MT5 is a bridge; Alpaca and CCXT are REST), no
execution desk measured against a benchmark, no regulator.

- **OMS/EMS process split · a FIX engine or QuickFIX · smart order routing · a TCA suite
  with VWAP or implementation-shortfall benchmarks · a separate risk process · RTS 25 clock
  synchronisation · CAT-style replay.** Preconditions absent.
- **Auto-liquidation on an automatic kill-switch trigger.** Stops rest at the broker; mass
  market-closing into the spread spike that caused the breach is its own failure mode.
- **Instrument-level volatility targeting.** ATR-denominated stops plus fixed-fractional
  risk (`strategy.py:253-258` + `calc_lots`) already make size inversely proportional to
  instrument vol. A per-symbol scalar double-counts and systematically under-sizes
  high-vol instruments. Portfolio-level vol targeting from the local `equity` table is the
  missing layer, and it is free — no network, satisfying NFR-1.
- **HMM / Markov-switching regime classifiers.** Unvalidatable at n=0 and a known
  overfitting magnet. Vol-ratio scalars survive out of sample because volatility is
  persistent and returns are not.
- **An estimated covariance matrix, or HRP.** O(N²) parameters against a sample that does
  not exist. A **hand-specified** 6×6 inter-bucket correlation prior is legitimate and
  catches the composite bet per-bucket caps miss (USD −1.9 plus RISK +1.9 passes both caps
  and is one ~3.8-unit macro bet).
- **Carver's IDM / diversification multiplier.** Forbidden by "factors may only reduce
  risk" and would be leverage built on an unmeasured correlation matrix (§6.3).
- **Supabase / Vercel hosted read model, or Tailscale Funnel** (§8).
- **vectorbt-style vectorised sweeps** until sweep time is actually a bottleneck.
- **Any `bypass` flag on any rail, in any environment, including backtest and conformance
  runs.** Nautilus ships `RiskEngineConfig(bypass=True)`; LEAN lets a plain `QCAlgorithm`
  call `MarketOrder` and skip the framework. Both are the natural next step when a test is
  awkward. **This repo has no such flag today. Record that absence as a decision, not an
  accident**, given that AI agents edit this code. If a test needs relaxed rails it
  constructs a different params snapshot; it never sets a flag the production path can read.

## 11. Amendments to commit alongside this document

### Add to `CLAUDE.md` safety invariants

- **I-A.** The engine only believes what the venue confirms. No local state advances on
  submission — not `tp1_done`, not `sl`, not `status`, not `lots`, not `pnl`.
- **I-B.** Submits are attempted **exactly once**. Ambiguity is resolved by **reading**
  (`order_by_client_id`, `recent_fills`, the position feed), never by writing again. Reads
  may retry with backoff; submits never.
- **I-C.** Every rail evaluates **one** params snapshot and **one** feed snapshot, both
  taken under their locks before the first check, and neither reachable by a strategy.
- **I-D.** Identity is the venue's ticket, captured from the ack. The order comment is a
  fallback hint the counterparty is free to rewrite.
- **I-E.** Protective stops rest at the broker. Therefore the dead-man switch **refuses
  stale work**; it does not flatten. An adapter whose stops are client-side must declare it
  (`supports_attached_stop`) and the engine must treat that venue differently.
- **I-F.** LIVE requires three independent yeses: strategy stage, named cell in
  `live_cells`, and the global master switch. The global switch may **veto** live; it may
  never **grant** it.
- **I-G.** No bypass flag on any rail, in any environment, including backtest and
  conformance.
- **I-H.** Rails live in `engine.py`. **Risk arithmetic** may live in a pure module both the
  engine and the dashboard import — the rail is *the decision to refuse*, and
  `engine.try_execute` remains its only caller. Write this down or a future reader will
  "fix" it back.
- **I-I.** `params_store` ceilings are the SEC 15c3-5 "direct and exclusive control"
  principle, correctly implemented and better than most retail systems. Defend them — and
  note they currently guard the **write layer** only; I-C closes the other half.

## 12. The one-line version

The architecture document designs for a problem this system does not have, on top of an
execution path that can double-fill, phantom-close, phantom-stop, silently widen a live
stop from an unauthenticated LAN endpoint, and mis-measure its own P&L — all on the only
venue that exists, all in a branch no test touches, all reachable by flipping one EA input,
and all keyed on a ticket number that wraps negative the first time you use a real account.

**But none of it is armed today, and the thing that actually gates going live is a paper
sample that has not started.** Spend this week on the seven changes in §1, start the clock,
and batch the execution work into one week immediately before you flip
`AllowTradeExecution` — when you will have real trades to test it with, and when the EA
changes ship as one round trip instead of five.

The promotion gate is this repo's best feature. Right now it scores the wrong quantity with
a broken ruler, at n=1, against a verdict file committed to git, and it voids itself every
time you halt for lunch. Fix the ruler before the sample, not after.
